Color

Primary color palette

Our new orange is vibrant and recognizable. It's a bold, accessible, and high-impact tone that aligns perfectly with our evolution and feels appetizing.

Coffee-themed, natural-toned neutrals fit into the system to support this vibrant orange and lend warmth while pairing well with food imagery.

Secondary colors

Our secondary colors are carefully chosen to complement our new orange by providing balance and versatility. They enhance the vibrancy of the orange while offering contrast that supports clarity and visual hierarchy.

Whether used to highlight key messaging or to create depth in layouts, these hues work harmoniously to reinforce Grubhub's energy and warmth without overwhelming it.
